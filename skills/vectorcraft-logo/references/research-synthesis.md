# Research behind the workflow

Primary sources reviewed 19 October 2026. These are attributed summaries and our practical
interpretations, not copied manuals or proof that one aesthetic works for every business.
Designer process accounts and case studies describe practice; they are not controlled evidence
that a logo causes sales, memorability or business growth. No proprietary artwork is included.

## Brief, exploration and selection

- [Paul Rand — Logos, Flags, and Escutcheons (1991)](https://www.paulrand.design/writing/articles/1991-logos-flags-and-escutcheons.html).
  Rand distinguishes identification from literal description and describes how association builds
  meaning. **Adopt:** do not require a logo to explain every service or hide several metaphors.
  **Limit:** his modernist position is one influential approach, not a mandate for geometric minimalism.
- [David Airey — Process](https://www.davidairey.com/process).
  Airey moves from business research to verbal/sketched directions, refinement and application
  demonstrations. **Adopt:** resolve the concept before investing in a complete kit; compare ideas
  against the brief. **Limit:** his typical concept counts, commercial stages and timelines are not
  requirements for an automated lead proposal or a minor adjustment.
- [Jessica Hische — Custom Art & Logo Commissions](https://www.jessicahische.is/available).
  Hische describes bespoke wordmarks developed through multiple directions and refinement for
  screen and print. **Adopt:** treat letterform design as a substantial identity route, rather than
  attaching default type to a symbol. **Limit:** custom lettering is an option, not an obligation.

## Typography and optical behaviour

- [Adobe — An introduction to kerning](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/design/discover/kerning.html).
  Pair spacing differs from whole-word tracking, and logo spacing needs application-specific
  inspection. **Adopt:** inspect troublesome pairs, then judge the word's overall rhythm at useful
  sizes. **Limit:** automatic optical kerning in Adobe is not evidence that VectorCraft offers the
  same command; inspect local capabilities. Do not generalise font quality from price.
- [Monotype / Charles Nix — What is optical sizing and how can it help your brand? (2019)](https://enterprise.monotype.com/resources/articles/what-is-optical-sizing-and-how-can-it-help-your-brand).
  Size-specific type designs adjust openings, spacing and detail for their context. **Adopt:**
  diagnose small-size failures and make labelled optical variants where useful. **Limit:** typeface
  optical-size ranges are not universal minimum logo sizes.
- [IBM Design Language — 8-Bar](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/8-bar/).
  IBM documents different stripe thicknesses and counter points in positive and reversed artwork.
  **Adopt:** visually compare light/dark variants rather than assuming a colour inversion is enough.
  **Limit:** do not transplant IBM's geometry, clear space or legal requirements to another identity.
- [Google Design — Evolving the Google Identity (2015)](https://design.google/library/evolving-google-identity).
  The identity uses related full and compact forms, including increased weight and optical refinement
  in the compact G. **Adopt:** derive compact variants from the identity and prove their useful
  sizes. **Limit:** these are lessons from the documented 2015 system, not current Google usage rules.

## Expression, systems and critique

- [COLLINS — Mailchimp](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/mailchimp/).
  The studio describes preserving idiosyncrasy through type, illustration, colour and imagery while
  building coherence. **Adopt:** allow expressive identities; a practical flat logo can coexist with
  richer supporting art. **Limit:** the studio's business-impact narrative does not establish that
  the identity alone caused commercial outcomes.
- [Pentagram — Viren](https://www.pentagram.com/work/viren).
  A symbol embedded in the wordmark also works across garment positions and variations. **Adopt:**
  test character replacements as readable type and as application assets. **Limit:** this is one
  successful strategy, not a reason to insert a symbol into every business name.
- [Mozilla — Heading into the home stretch (2016)](https://blog.mozilla.org/opendesign/heading-into-the-home-stretch/).
  Mozilla describes criteria-led critique and qualitative questioning to uncover confusion rather
  than declare a popularity winner. **Adopt:** ask what the work communicates and why, then revise
  visible problems. **Limit:** internal model critique is not a substitute for real audience research;
  do not invent user-testing results or claim recall from an untested design.

## How the sources are reconciled

Use clarity and reproducibility to test whether an identity works in its intended setting. Keep
expressive character when it supports that setting. A richly drawn emblem may suit a shop sign;
a derived compact form may serve its mobile header. Neither extreme detail nor extreme reduction
is a default quality standard. The concept and review reference turns this synthesis into decisions;
it is our workflow, not a verbatim method attributed to any single designer.
